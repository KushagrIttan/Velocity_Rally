# First Velocity Rally playtest

Recruit 8–12 consenting testers: a mix of casual players, racing players, and people who mainly play on touch devices. This worksheet is ready for use; no humans have been recruited or observed yet. Do not coach a tester through the first attempt. Record anonymous observations unless a tester agrees to attribution.

## Session (15–20 minutes)

1. Ask them to choose a car and play First Tracks, then Fern Valley. Observe whether they understand braking, surfaces, recovery, and camera controls.
2. After their first finish, stop talking. Watch whether they choose another attempt. Let them make at least three runs if they want to.
3. Ask them to describe one mistake they can improve. Observe whether their next run changes that behavior.
4. Ask them to select another unlocked route, alter volume/graphics, and locate their best ghost.
5. On a shared test server, have two testers submit runs under agreed test names, copy a ghost link, and race the cyan rival. Tell them their run and name will appear on that server.
6. Invite them to return the next day for the daily course. Do not send reminders unless they explicitly agree to a channel and time.

Ask: “What made you retry or stop?”, “Where did the car surprise you?”, “What would you try differently next run?”, and “Would you choose to play this again tomorrow?” Prefer observed behavior to a satisfaction score.

## Iteration decisions

- If players cannot finish, adjust onboarding, corner entry speed, steering, recovery, and bronze targets before adding content.
- If they finish but do not retry, improve the ghost/next-target feedback and the stage's satisfying corner sequence.
- If clean driving does not improve time, investigate handling, scoring, or course readability.
- If they repeatedly earn gold on their first try, tighten gold while keeping bronze approachable.
- If progress is blocked for competent players, tune silver/bronze separately by stage.
- Profile the device on which someone reports stutter; avoid guessing from a development machine.

Use aggregate, consented observations. No analytics service or automatic data collection is installed in the game.
